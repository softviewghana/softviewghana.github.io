# **Software Products Built to Solve Real Problems**

We don't only build software for clients. We develop and own digital products designed to address practical challenges and create lasting value.

Every product starts with a problem worth solving. We focus on understanding the people, processes, and everyday challenges behind that problem, then build software around those needs.

**Featured product **

## **eSchool Ghana **

**The School Operating System for basic schools in Ghana. **

**Planned launch January 2027 **

Running a school involves coordinating students, teachers, academics, finances, communication, and administration. eSchool Ghana is being developed to bring these essential operations together in one connected platform.

### **Product capabilities **

Admissions & Enrollment

Student Information Management

Academic Administration

Attendance Management

Assessments & Examinations

Automatic Report Cards

Fees & Payment Management

Accounting & Financial Administration

Staff & Payroll Administration

SMS, Email & Telegram Communication

Parent & Teacher Portals

Timetables & School Calendar

Reports & Analytics

**Product direction **

### **Built around how schools actually operate. **

The goal is to give school stakeholders a more connected way to manage everyday responsibilities and access the information they need.

#### **One Integrated Platform**

Bring essential school operations together instead of relying on disconnected tools and manual processes.

#### **Better Operational Visibility**

Help school leaders access relevant information across academic, administrative, and financial activities.

#### **Designed Around School Workflows**

Built with the operational realities of private basic schools in Ghana in mind.

#### **Connected School Community**

Support coordination between administrators, teachers, parents, and other school stakeholders.

#### **Room to Grow**

A flexible foundation designed to support schools as their operations and requirements evolve.

## **EPSSP **

**Electronic Payroll & Staff Self-Service Portal **

EPSSP simplifies employee records, payroll processing, statutory deductions, payslips and staff self-service for organisations across Ghana.

Human Resources Payroll Employee Self-Service

- Better visibility into staff administration
