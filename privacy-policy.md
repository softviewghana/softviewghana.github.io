# **Privacy Policy**

How Softview Ghana collects, uses, protects, and manages information.

**Last Updated:** August 10, 2026

## **1. Introduction**

Softview Ghana is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who interact with our website, products, and services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard information in connection with our work.

## **2. Information We Collect **

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, organization, and project details when you contact us or request information about our services or products. We may also collect technical information automatically, such as IP address, device details, browser type, page visits, and referral sources.

## **3. How We Use Information **

We use information to respond to inquiries, assess project requirements, provide information about our products and services, improve our website experience, maintain security, and support business operations. We do not use personal information for unrelated marketing purposes without your consent.

## **4. Cookies and Similar Technologies**

We may use cookies and similar technologies to understand website usage, improve performance, and manage basic analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the site may not work as intended if cookies are disabled.

## **5. Analytics**

We may use analytics tools to collect information about visitor behavior and site performance. This helps us understand which content and pages are useful and improve the user experience without compromising privacy.

## **6. Communications**

If you contact us, we may communicate with you by email, phone, or other appropriate channels to answer your enquiry, share information, and continue project-related discussions. You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

## **7. Data Sharing**

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our website and business, such as hosting, analytics, or communication tools, only where necessary and under appropriate confidentiality obligations.

## **8. Data Security**

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## **9. Data Retention**

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

## **10. Your Rights**

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of personal information. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

## **11. Third-Party Services **

Our website or services may link to third-party resources or use third-party tools that have their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those external services.

## **12. Children's Privacy**

Our website and services are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent or legal basis.

## **13. International Data Transfers**

Information may be processed or stored in locations outside your home jurisdiction. Where required, we will take appropriate safeguards for international data transfers.

## **14. Changes to This Policy **

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any material changes will be posted on this page with the updated date so you can review the latest version.

## **15. Contact Us**

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle personal information, please contact us at hello@softviewghana.com.
